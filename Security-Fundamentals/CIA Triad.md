### Confidentiality

- Only authorized people can access the information /data.
- Controls:
    - Encryption
    - Authentication
    - Authorization

### Integrity

- Information must remain accurate and unaltered unless authorized.
- Controls:
    - Hashing
    - Digital Signatures
    - Change Management
    - Access Contol

### Availability

- System/data should be accessible when legitimate users need them.
- Controls:
    - Backups
    - Load Balancers
    - Redundancy
